# 1000 a 100000 — Portfolio + Context Policy V1

Effective from historical J9 shadow work and future live slates.

## 1. Weekly real-money envelope

- Maximum planned real exposure: MXN 200 per week.
- Target portfolio: 3 two-leg parlays.
- Two conservative tickets + one aggressive ticket.
- Every parlay leg must come from a different match; no same-game/interrelated legs.
- Default stake guide: MXN 80 conservative A + MXN 80 conservative B + MXN 40 aggressive.
- Larger odds receive smaller stake. The default split is an execution heuristic, not a model-promotion criterion.
- The MXN 200 is a ceiling, not a quota. If three positive-edge, context-cleared tickets do not exist, leave capital unbet rather than force a wager.
- REAL_USER_OVERRIDE remains excluded from scientific promotion evidence.

## 2. Meaning of conservative / aggressive

Conservative does NOT mean merely low bookmaker odds. It means:
- high model probability relative to the available market;
- low uncertainty / well-supported market family;
- no material context contradiction;
- no excessive portfolio concentration;
- minimum execution odds clear the preregistered price gate.

Aggressive means:
- lower hit probability / higher variance and generally higher payout;
- still requires positive model-vs-market edge and context clearance;
- receives lower stake;
- never exists merely to satisfy a desire for a long shot.

## 3. Mandatory context layer

For every historical and forward candidate, freeze MODEL_SIGNAL first, then audit only information available before that fixture's context cutoff.

Context fields to inspect when evidence exists:
- expected/confirmed lineups;
- injuries and suspensions;
- continuity of relevant players and tactical roles;
- manager/system changes;
- rest days, travel and schedule congestion;
- stadium, surface, altitude and home-specific pattern;
- kickoff time and weather when demonstrably relevant;
- referee identity and referee tendencies only if backed by a pre-cutoff historical sample;
- recent tactical/form pattern not already fully represented in score-only state;
- comparable H2H, with recency/personnel/system caveats.

Every context item is tagged:
- SUPPORT
- NEUTRAL
- CONTRADICT_LOW
- CONTRADICT_HIGH
- UNKNOWN / TIMING_UNCERTAIN

Until context features have their own validated numeric model, context does NOT arbitrarily add percentage points to p_model. It may confirm, downgrade, quarantine or veto a candidate. A CONTEXT hypothesis can enter the architecture-proposal queue and later become a feature only after untouched historical + forward validation.

## 4. Expanded market universe

Eligible to COMPUTE/LOG when generated by a validated mathematical readout:
- 1X2
- double chance
- match totals
- team totals
- BTTS
- score-matrix-compatible markets
- total corners
- team corners
- corner-result markets

Goal markets are derived from the Dixon-Coles score matrix.
Corners are modeled separately by C0.1-CORNERS-NB-ENSEMBLE (Negative Binomial), SHADOW ONLY until promoted.

No market receives a guaranteed portfolio slot. Corners compete against goal markets on evidence. We do not force a corner bet for variety.

## 5. Historical replay integrity

- Preserve the canonical V0.1R replay so J1 onward stays comparable.
- Expanded markets and the new three-ticket/two-leg weekly portfolio run as a separate SHADOW branch beginning at J9.
- Historical Caliente odds remain incomplete. Therefore historical replay may assess probability quality, calibration and settlement, but may not claim historical EV/ROI/CLV without contemporaneous odds.
- Current forward tickets require house odds, timestamp, de-vig/market probability where feasible, model edge, minimum odds and context audit.

## 6. Portfolio construction

For each weekly/slate shadow portfolio:
1. Generate all eligible atomic legs.
2. Apply calibration/support/uncertainty gates.
3. Run mandatory context audit.
4. Remove negative-edge or context-vetoed legs.
5. Build two conservative two-leg tickets and one aggressive two-leg ticket, each using different matches within the ticket.
6. Audit repeated exposure across tickets and reduce concentration when a comparably strong alternative exists.
7. Freeze before outcomes.
8. Settle append-only and classify errors. A single win/loss never changes architecture.
